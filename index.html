WordQuest - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Overview

WordQuest is an offline word puzzle game intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Information We Collect

WordQuest does not require account registration. The game itself does not request your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Local Game Data

Game progress, coins, stars, daily challenges, and preferences are stored locally on your device. You can erase this information using the game's reset option or by uninstalling the application.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

WordQuest uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded, interstitial, and banner ads.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location, device information, and ad interactions to provide, secure, and measure advertisements.

For more information, visit Google Privacy Policy.

Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required, WordQuest requests advertising consent through Google's consent management system. Users may be offered options to accept, reject, or manage consent. Applicable privacy options will be accessible within the application.

Children's Privacy

WordQuest is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed toward children.

Data Security

Game progress is stored locally on your device. Advertising data is processed by Google and its partners according to their applicable policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our application or data practices change. Updates will be reflected on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact us using the developer contact information provided on the WordQuest Google Play listing.
